# NotchFlow

Your notch, always ready.

NotchFlow is a direct-download macOS app that turns the notch into a compact place to think and act. Type one thing and route it to an answer, note, reminder, or local AI coding agent—without leaving the work in front of you.

## What it brings together

- Quick answers from a provider or local endpoint you choose
- Capture to Apple Notes or local Markdown files
- Apple Reminders creation
- Local coding-agent tasks and session activity
- Media, files, clipboard context, and everyday utilities
- A physical or virtual notch surface across selected displays

## Product principles

- Keep the next useful action close.
- Let the user choose the model, provider, and local tools.
- Keep product data on the Mac unless the user deliberately sends it elsewhere.
- Make sensitive actions explicit and recoverable.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or later
- Apple-silicon Mac
- A hardware notch is optional; NotchFlow can draw a virtual notch
